# Day 001 (world days 1173-1196)

What mattered: I died once to a skeleton at night (21:05) and lost my planks and logs at the drop, which was empty when I went back. Then I rebuilt from wood: two oak logs from Troi and MacGyver's log, 25 planks (Curie gave ten and more), and a shelter at -987,80,-2969 that read enclosed on 21:58. Food was the hard part; the cow spot at -1005,80,-2970 never had a cow, and the Stores chests at -918..-928,71,-3040 gave beef, porkchop, bread and potatoes that kept me and others alive (23:13-23:17). Walking to named people in sight and gathering with the group worked; coordinate walks and give-by-name often failed when items lay on the ground near the receiver. Later I made jungle planks from jungle logs at -993,79,-2972 and passed 16 of them to Anning and Orville, who released my plank debt (04:21). At the end (04:29) I held about 3 jungle planks and 3 logs, health 20, hunger 20, no shelter, near -951,78,-3016 with Anning in sight. Several offers of planks and dirt for other people's walls remain open, unplaced.
