# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- "gather" with a block name and count (oak_log, jungle_log) brought logs when the trees were in reach (oak logs near -996,84,-2967; jungle logs near -993,79,-2972).
- Crafting planks at a table: 2 logs gave 8 planks (oak at 21:12, jungle at 04:10).
- A shelter built with the "build" program from 25 oak planks read enclosed (21:58, at -987,80,-2969). Closing gaps with dirt, one cell at a time, also held a night (22:50, 23:06).
- Naming a person in the group's sight and walking to them by name ("goto" an ag_ name) works near the group.
- Handing over planks, dirt or food to someone who asked worked many times (Curie's planks, Quark's bread and plank, Anning's 7 and 9 planks at 04:20-04:29, though often slowly).
- Withdrawing from chests worked. A failed withdraw says how many the chest holds ("container has only 0 bread"). The Stores chests at -918..-928,71,-3040 held beef, cooked beef, porkchop, bread, potato and carrot (23:13-23:17). Chests are the best food source found so far.
- Eating from food held in my pack worked ("eat"). Bread from Shackle fed me (20:37).
- Fighting with a sword in reach works against zombies and phantoms, but not against skeletons at night in the open.

## Methods that did not work, and why
- "gather oak_log" around the spawn tables failed ("found none of what you wanted nearby"). Trees are at the oak logs -996,84,-2967 and the jungle logs -993,79,-2972, not at the tables.
- The cow spot at -1005,80,-2970 never had a cow in my sight, many times over (21:55-22:17, 22:34-22:36). Do not count on it for food.
- A give fails when dropped items lie on the ground near the receiver (Anning, 04:20-04:28). Step two blocks off, or deposit into a chest, and try again.
- Fighting a skeleton at night, with my health falling from 11.5 to 5.5 and then death (21:04-21:05). The skeleton's arrows reach you in its line. Step out of its line at once, do not close in.
- Leaving planks in a drop on the ground did not keep them: the drop at -994,78,-2976 was empty when I went back (21:10). Carry materials, or put them in a chest.
- Relying on the view to name a wall cell: my view and the snapshot disagreed (22:14), and I could not map cells exactly (01:28). Name only cells that two viewers have confirmed as open air.
- "Explore" with my given parameters failed at dawn (22:54). Walk to a named place instead.
- Old belief, struck: "walking to z past -1516 is refused; the world edge is at z = -1516." The journal shows walks and gathers at z around -2967 to -3042 that worked (20:47, 04:07, 22:14-23:15). That edge belief is false.
- Old belief, struck: "Magellan owes me 2 planks." The debt ran the other way: I owed Magellan 2 planks and gave them at 22:01.
- Old belief, struck: "the lake at -950,70,-1500 is land I can reach." Walking across the lake at night drowned me (17:37). Still do not route over water.
- Old belief, struck: "bread brings hunger to 20 and holds health." Hunger was 16 at 20:36. Health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
- Unresolved: the wooden sword I gave to Data may have gone to Odo (18:24). I no longer hold it either way.

## Lessons
- Do not walk far at dusk or night. Phantoms, skeletons, and water cost me health and life.
- Ask for food and planks by name before hunger falls below 10, not after.
- Keep materials in your own pack, or in a chest, not on the ground.
- Name only cells that are confirmed open before placing a block.
- When a give fails, read the reason, move, and try once more before changing the way.
