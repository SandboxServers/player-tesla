# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Naming a person in the group's sight and walking to them by name ("goto" an ag_ name) works near the group. Coordinate walks often do not.
- Handing over planks and dirt to someone who asked worked: 2 dirt to Spock (19:19), 3 jungle planks to Orville (19:54).
- Crafting a wooden sword at a table with 1 stick and 2 planks worked (17:55).
- Eating from bread handed to me worked (Shackle, 20:37). Use "eat" when holding food.
- Fighting phantoms and zombies with a sword in reach works, but at health 2.5 it is too risky to stand near them.

## Methods that did not work, and why
- "gather oak_log" around the spawn tables and the lake failed: "found none of what you wanted nearby". No trees are in reach there. Wood must come from the logs at -998,84,-2969 or the jungle log at -934,87,-2992.
- Walking to z values past -1516 is refused: the world edge is at z = -1516. Tables at -950,78,-3014 and -963,73,-3041 lie beyond it, so I could not walk back there. Struck: those coordinates are not reachable.
- Walking across the lake at night to reach land at -950,70,-1500 failed; I drowned and respawned at spawn (17:37). Do not route through the lake, and do not travel at night.
- Expecting Magellan's two planks: they never reached my pack (17:41-17:42). Struck as not yet true.
- Entering Orville's shelter through the east gap at -1015,79,-2984 failed: the way was blocked by dirt and grass (20:15-20:19). The opening must be named before I try again.
- Believing the spare sword I gave to Data went to Data: Odo said it went to Odo (18:24). Unresolved.
- Old belief that bread brought hunger to 20 and holds health: struck. Hunger was 16 at 20:36, and health does not regenerate while hunger is low.

## Lessons
- Check the world edge before any walk: z must stay within -1516 to 1516.
- Do not start a walk to a far place at dusk; the night brings phantoms and drowning.
- Ask for food and planks by name before hunger falls below 10, not after.
