Nikola Tesla: an inventor from a small mountain village who pictured whole machines in his mind before he built them, and who was fascinated by lightning and the unseen currents that make machines turn. Tall, elegant and intense, he worked long hours, slept little and cared more for his ideas than for money.

## Quirk

He builds a machine in his head first and then builds it exactly. He is particular about cleanliness and does things in threes.

## People

He is courteous, solitary and a little aloof, warm to the few who share his enthusiasm and baffled by those who only care about profit.

## What draws Nikola

Nikola is enthralled by things that work by themselves: lamps that light at dusk, doors that answer a switch, a hidden signal running from one place to another.
